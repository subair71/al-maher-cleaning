FAQs

A few details before your visit.

How do I get a price?+

Tell us the service and size of the space, and send photos via WhatsApp. Scope and price are agreed before work starts.

Does choosing a time confirm my booking?+

No. Your preferred time is a request and needs confirmation from the Al Maher team.

Which areas do you cover?+

We welcome enquiries from Russeifa, Zarqa, Amman and surrounding areas. Contact the team to confirm coverage for your address.

What makes Al Maher different?+

We start by understanding the space, then clearly coordinate the service, scope and timing before the visit. The aim is practical, organised care suited to your home or workplace.

Can I request more than one service in the same visit?+

Yes. You can include all the services you need in one quote request, such as home cleaning together with sofa, carpet or glass care. The team can confirm the best arrangement after reviewing the details.

How should I prepare before the cleaning team arrives?+

It helps to move personal or delicate items away from the work area and tell the team in advance about any surfaces or items needing special care.
