First-version website proposal · Prepared for discussion · 30 September 2026

Price, dates, hosting, service levels and ownership terms below require written agreement. The current website is an interactive demonstration; production setup is a separate implementation stage.

01 / First-version scope

Customer website

  • Premium mobile, tablet and desktop design using the official unchanged logo and blue/gold identity.
  • Arabic-first RTL and complete English LTR content and forms, with saved language choice.
  • Home, About, six existing service detail pages, offers, reviews, contact, FAQs, draft Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • Before-and-after comparison gallery with service categories; approved customer photos and reviews supplied by the client.
  • WhatsApp and call links for +962778842130, existing address, verified Google Maps pin and opening hours when supplied.
  • Quote requests and booking requests with preferred date/time; bookings require team confirmation.
  • Multiple photo attachments with previews, validation, removal and progress.

Production administration & operations

  • Secure administrator authentication and protected server access; service, gallery, offer and review management.
  • Search/filter quote and booking records, view attachments and update processing status.
  • Server-side validation, spam controls, private uploads and access rules for customer information.
  • Page metadata, sitemap, robots directives, language metadata and appropriate business structured data.
  • Google Analytics setup after the client provides an ID and approves consent requirements.
  • HTTPS/SSL configuration and scheduled backups once the hosting platform and maintenance terms are agreed.
  • Source-code handover, configuration notes and an administrator walkthrough under agreed handover terms.

05 / Maintenance, backup & support

Proposed initial support: 30 calendar days after accepted launch for reproducible defects within the agreed scope. This duration, scope and response targets require agreement. Ongoing updates, content changes and monitoring need a separate maintenance plan and price.

Proposed support channel: agreed email or WhatsApp contact. Proposed acknowledgement within two business days; critical access/data incidents targeted within one business day during agreed support hours. Business hours, holidays, escalation and restoration commitments are to be confirmed. No 24/7 or uptime guarantee is included.

Proposed backup policy: encrypted nightly database and uploaded-photo backups, 30 daily restore points plus 3 monthly snapshots, protected from the production account’s routine access. Automated failure alerts and quarterly restore exercises. Provider support, storage cost and maintenance responsibility must be confirmed. Target maximum data loss: 24 hours; target restoration: one business day during support coverage, subject to validated backup size and provider capability. These are proposed targets, not an SLA.

06 / Exclusions & future options

Not included in version one: customer accounts, loyalty points/rewards software, recurring subscription billing, team scheduling or staff assignment, online payments, electronic invoices, mobile apps, paid advertising, ongoing SEO/content campaigns, guaranteed rankings, professional photography and legal advice. These can be scoped and priced separately. Existing promotional copy about repeat customers does not include a loyalty system.

Marketing results, cleaning results and confirmed service availability are business responsibilities. Any extra service, page family, language or integration beyond the agreed scope requires a change estimate.

07 / Proposed ownership & handover

Subject to a signed agreement and final payment, the proposal is to hand over the custom website source code, build/deployment instructions, schema/API documentation, administrator training and agreed repository access or transfer. Third-party libraries, stock photographs, fonts, hosting and services remain subject to their own licences and terms.

Client-provided branding and content remain the client’s property. The proposed production arrangement gives the client control of business customer data and export access. Hosting, domain, analytics and administrator accounts should be client-owned, with developer access delegated and reviewed at handover. Exact IP assignment, repository transfer, retention/deletion obligations and credentials handover must be agreed; no ownership guarantee is made here. Passwords and secrets must never be committed to the repository.
